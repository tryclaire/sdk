# Changelog

## 0.7.0

Tokens already live on pons can be brought to Claire (`ClaireFeeRouter.adopt`, with a USD 25 migration fee); the protocol verifiers recognise them. The creator can now raise a router's holder share at any time (never lower it) and change the payee with 7 days' public notice; the verifiers follow those changes. Breaking: the router, router factory and proof view ABIs changed (the factory constructor takes `migrationFee` after `launchFee` and a `ClaireRouterDeployer` last; routers and proof view read `payee` and `holderBps` as current values).

- ABIs and test artifacts are synced from the updated protocol build (`ClaireFeeRouter` adds `adopt`, `migrationFee`, `historicalVest`, the `Adopted`, `MigrationFeePaid` and `HistoricalFeesPaid` events and the `UnsweptFees` error; `ClaireRouterFactory` adds `migrationFee`).
- ABIs gain `ClaireRouterDeployer` (the router's creation code lives there, so the factory stays under the size limit; `ClaireRouterFactory.routers()` returns it). `ClaireFeeRouter` adds `raiseHolderShare`, `proposePayee`, `cancelPayee`, `applyPayee`, `pendingPayee`, `payeeChangeAt`, `PAYEE_DELAY` (7 days), the `HolderShareRaised`, `PayeeChangeProposed`, `PayeeChangeCanceled` and `PayeeChanged` events and the `NoPendingPayee` and `PayeeChangeNotReady` errors. Every router now has a project holder distributor, including at a 0% holder share.
- `ClaireDeployment` gains `routerDeployer` and `block` (the manifest's pre-deployment chain height). The verifiers' event scans default `fromBlock` to the chain's `deployments` entry's `block` instead of 0 (still 0 for an unlisted chain; the `verifyRound` token-transfer scan keeps 0 because the token can predate Claire).
- `ClaireSplit` adds `release(asset, index)`, which pays one payee's share (`InvalidIndex` and `TransferFailed` errors); ABIs resynced.
- `readRouter` adds `migrationFee` and `historicalVest` (the creator's share of a buyback vest inherited at adoption that is not yet paid out), plus `pendingPayee` and `payeeChangeAt`; `payee` and `holderBps` are the values now in effect. `readProof` returns the same two new fields.
- `verifyLaunch` recognises adopted tokens and reports `facts.path`: `'launched'` (one `Launched` event) or `'migrated'` (one `Adopted` event and one `MigrationFeePaid` payment by the creator matching the router's `migrationFee`, pons only). Both paths must show the router registering the token (`ClaireLaunchRegistered`), and the router's `migrationFee` is checked against the factory's. `facts.launchBlock` is the block of the `Launched` or `Adopted` event.
- `verifyLaunch` predicts the router's address through the factory's `ClaireRouterDeployer` (optional `routerDeployer` input, default the factory's own) from the `RouterDeployed` event's initial values. The payee and holder share may differ from that event only through the router's own events: `HolderShareRaised`, `PayeeChangeProposed`, `PayeeChangeCanceled` and `PayeeChanged` are replayed (raises only go up, a payee change follows a proposal made 7 days earlier) and must end at the router's current values and pending proposal (`split-history` check). `facts.initial`, `facts.holderShareRaises` and `facts.payeeChanges` report the history; a payee or holder share that differs without an event fails.
- `verifyRouterSplit` computes each distribution at the holder share in effect when it happened (events replayed in log order, `split-history` check) and reports `facts.holderShareRaises`, `facts.payeeChanges` and the payee of each distribution (`facts.payees`).
- `verifyRouterSplit` reads `HistoricalFeesPaid` into `facts.historical`: fees and vest earned before adoption are paid to the creator uncharged, are not revenue, and stay out of `revenue` and `totalRevenue`.
- The canonical round manifest format is `version: 1`: `parseRoundManifest` and `verifyRound` accept version 1 only.
- API client: fee routers carry `pendingPayee`, `payeeChangeAt` and `payeeChanges` (the holder share and payee are the current values, with their confirmed history) and `token.proof.get()` reports `launch.pendingPayee`; the bundled OpenAPI snapshot and generated types are resynced.
- API client: fee routers carry `migrated` and `migration` (adoption time, migration fee and the pre-migration fees paid to the creator uncharged), and `token.proof.get()` reports `launch.migrated`. The bundled OpenAPI snapshot and generated types are updated.
- API client: `token.proof.get()`'s `url` is now the token's permalink (`/proof/<chainId>/<token>`), which opens the project's public site where the same facts are shown; `url + "/badge.svg"` is still the badge. Descriptions in the OpenAPI snapshot and generated types are updated.

## 0.6.0

`@tryclaire/sdk/protocol` now targets the Claire protocol contracts. Breaking: the distributor ABI, leaf encoding and round manifest changed, and the router and token lock ABIs gained fields.

- API client: added `token.proof.get()` for `GET /token/proof` (the facts of the public Claire Proof card, read from the chain; `data` is null when no token is linked). Lock rows now cover `kind: "time" | "revenue" | "price"` with `cancelNotice` and price-target fields, fee routers carry `payee`, `payeeKind`, `payeeTerms`, `buyback` and per-asset totals including launch-token revenue, and rounds are cumulative (`cumulativeTotal`, `previousEvidence`). The bundled OpenAPI snapshot and generated types are updated.
- ABIs for all 11 contracts (adds `ClaireVestingVault`, `ClaireSplit`, `ClaireVaultFactory`, `ClairePriceLock`, `ClaireProofView` and `ClaireDistributorFactory`).
- `verifyRouterSplit` counts launch-token (buyback) revenue next to quote revenue and fails revenue charged in any other asset. `readRouter` adds `payee`, `buybackVault`, `stockereumPoolId` and `revenueToken`. `verifyLaunch` checks the payee, buyback vault and Stockereum pool id against the factory and its events, and with `vaultFactory` classifies the payee (`facts.payeeKind`).
- New verifiers `verifyPriceLock`, `verifyVestingVault` and `verifySplit`, and readers `readPriceLock`, `readVestingVault`, `readSplit` and `readProof` (`ClaireProofView`).
- `verifyLock` checks the notice-period cancel state (`facts.cancelState`, `cancelNotice`, `cancelAt`) against the `CancelRequested` and `Canceled` events.
- `verifyRound` verifies cumulative distributor roots and the cumulative manifest (`previousEvidence` chaining, per-account cumulative continuity, `cumulativeTotal`, claims within the latest root) and takes an optional `previousManifest`. `roundLeafHash` is now `roundLeafHash(chainId, distributor, asset, { account, cumulative })`, `RoundLeaf` is `{ account, cumulative }`, and `parseRoundManifest` accepts the cumulative manifest only.
- `ClaireDeployment` carries every address of the deploy manifest (`routerFactory`, `tokenLock`, `revenueLock`, `priceLock`, `distributorFactory`, `vaultFactory`, `proofView`, `claireHolderDistributor`, `referralDistributor`, `treasury`, `feeToken`); the `deployments` map stays empty until deployment.

## 0.5.0

- Added `token.locks.list()`, `token.feeRouters.list()` and `token.rounds.list()` for the new `GET /token/locks`, `/token/fee-routers` and `/token/rounds` reads (`token:read`). Lock rows cover both time locks and revenue locks (`kind: "time" | "revenue"`) with their public proof IDs, and each round carries the URL of its canonical manifest. The bundled OpenAPI snapshot and generated types are updated.
- Added `@tryclaire/sdk/protocol`: typed contract ABIs, readers (`readLaunch`, `readRouter`, `readLock`, `readRevenueLock`, `readRound`), verifiers (`verifyLaunch`, `verifyRouterSplit`, `verifyLock`, `verifyRevenueLock`, `verifyRound`) and hashing helpers (`roundLeafHash`, `merkleRoot`, `merkleProof`, `manifestEvidence`) that check Claire launches, locks and distribution rounds against a chain you provide.
- `viem` is an optional peer dependency used only by the `protocol` subpath. The root API client is unchanged and dependency-free.
